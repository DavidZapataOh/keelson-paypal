# Security

> **Unaudited.** The contracts have had internal review, static analysis, fuzzing and partial formal verification, not an external audit. ERC-8004 is a Draft. The deployment is on testnets (Arbitrum Sepolia, with an Ethereum Sepolia address mirror); an Arbitrum One deployment, when made, holds small amounts only and covers no live purchases.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report privately through GitHub's security advisories for this repository ("Report a vulnerability"). Do not open a public issue. We answer within 72 hours.

## Threat model

| Actor                                                                  | Can                                                                                                           | Cannot                                                                                                        |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Operator (agent owner or agent wallet)                                 | Sign coverages, withdrawals and validation requests for its agent                                             | Release a coverage before `releaseAfter`, withdraw locked or queued funds, slash or rate itself               |
| Merchant                                                               | Set its payout address, claim failed payouts                                                                  | Slash without a verdict both TEE legs signed                                                                  |
| TEE legs (Intel TDX, AWS Nitro)                                        | Countersign purchases, sign verdicts and checkpoints                                                          | Act without an allowed measurement and a live registration; one leg alone slashes only through the 72 h queue |
| Relayer and anchor submitter                                           | Pay gas for allowlisted calls                                                                                 | Hold any role or change what a signed message says                                                            |
| Guardian Safe (2 of 3)                                                 | Pause new coverage, veto a queued slash, revoke a signer, disallow a measurement, cancel a timelock operation | Move bonded or reserve funds                                                                                  |
| Timelock (sole admin)                                                  | Change parameters, allow measurements, set roles after its delay                                              | Bypass the AccessManager's execution delays                                                                   |
| External: PYUSD, ERC-8004 registries, Automata DCAP, `nitro-validator` | Pause, freeze, upgrade or change collateral under their own owners                                            | See "Trust assumptions"                                                                                       |

## Trust assumptions

- Both TEE legs run the allowed measurements and refuse to sign a request id twice; the allowlist changes only through the Timelock.
- PYUSD's owner and asset protector act lawfully. A frozen vault cannot pay anyone; `wipeFrozenAddress` on the vault would leave it insolvent.
- The ERC-8004 Identity registry is honest about agent ownership: withdrawals are authorized by `ownerOf` and `getAgentWallet` (see M-5).
- Arbitrum's sequencer orders transactions and its clock stays within its documented bounds (up to 24 h behind, 1 h ahead).

## Findings

The deployed vault, reserve and verifier are immutable and bound to the TEE legs and to each other, so findings in them are recorded with their mitigation and fixed in the next release.

| Id  | Severity | Finding                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Status                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| --- | -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| M-1 | Medium   | `lockCoverage` checks `expiresAt >= cs.issuedAt + disputeWindow` rather than against the block time. A lock sent late in the countersign's life can leave a dispute window as short as `grace`, and a clean release still earns a +1.00 rating. | Next release: `expiresAt >= max(block.timestamp, issuedAt) + disputeWindow`, as `lockCoverageBatch` does. Until then the short window is visible on chain (`CoverageLocked` carries `releaseAfter`), so readers can discount such coverage. |
| M-2 | Medium   | A purchase locked and released as its own coverage can then be placed in a batch, where `slashBatched` refuses it (`NotInBatch`). The batch root is signed by the operator only.                                                                | Next release: batch slashes regardless of the individual coverage, or leaves proven against the anchored log. Until then batches are not used for live purchases.                                                                           |
| M-3 | Medium   | A verdict submitted with only its TDX signature is treated as degraded and queued for 72 h; the 2-of-2 copy then reverts as already settled.                                                                                                    | Next release: a 2-of-2 submission upgrades a queued degraded slash. Until then the effect is a 72 h delay of the merchant's payout and of the agent's withdrawals, not a loss.                                                              |
| M-4 | Medium   | `KeelsonReserve.requestCooldown` can be repeated, so a staker can keep a near-permanent exit window and leave ahead of a warranty.                                                                                                              | Next release: an unstake window shorter than the cooldown. Testnet parameters (10 min cooldown, 24 h window).                                                                                                                               |
| M-5 | Medium   | Withdrawals are authorized by the ERC-8004 Identity registry, which its owner can upgrade; there is no veto on a withdrawal.                                                                                                                    | Next release: Guardian-vetoable withdrawals. Release and slash paths never call the registry.                                                                                                                                               |
| L-1 | Low      | TEE messages are not bound to a vault, and a second vault on the same verifier would accept them. Each vault still needs the operator's own coverage signature.                                                                                 | The preview vault holds no funds.                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| L-2 | Low      | The Guardian can `disallowMeasurement` without delay, which stops new slashes and anchors until the Timelock allows a measurement again. Queued slashes still execute.                                                                          | Documented; intended as the emergency stop for a compromised enclave.                                                                                                                                                                       |
| L-3 | Low      | `executeSlash` consumes `min(amount, capacity)`: a zero capacity removes the limit, and a slash above capacity waits for a full bucket.                                                                                                         | Parameters are timelocked.                                                                                                                                                                                                                  |
| L-4 | Low      | Once size S' is anchored, a smaller size can never be, so a verdict naming it fails until re-signed.                                                                                                                                            | The legs re-sign against the latest anchor.                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| L-5 | Low      | `slashed[id]` is shared by batch leaves and individual coverages; reusing an id leaves the later coverage underpaid or locked.                                                                                                                  | Requires the legs to sign a reused id, which they refuse.                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| L-6 | Low      | `VerdictVerifier._admit` does not reject signer `address(0)`.                                                                                                                                                                                   | Unreachable without an allowed enclave binding key zero.                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| L-7 | Low      | The on-chain passkey check binds the key to the operator's coverage, not to the user; user consent rests on the legs. `Countersign.merchantId` is not compared with `Coverage.merchant`.                                                        | Payouts read only the TEE-signed `Verdict.payee`.                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| L-8 | Low      | Relayer and API: an authorized client can relay tiny deposits at Keelson's gas cost; the deposit key omits `from`; a permit key is the signature's hash; fallback RPCs that disagree can re-send a dropped call.                                | Clients are authenticated and rate-limited; every relayed call is idempotent on chain.                                                                                                                                                      |
| —   | Low      | `lockCoverage` accepts a coverage id already slashed as a batch leaf; `KeelsonReserve.maxDeposit`/`maxMint` ignore the cooldown.                                                                                                                | As above (L-5); no funds at risk.                                                                                                                                                                                                           |

## Known limitations

- Gas: several paths exceed their design targets and are held at their measured values (see the README's gas budgets).
- `KeelsonBondVault` is 238 bytes under EIP-170; new vault features go in separate contracts.
- Formal verification is partial: the open Certora properties are listed in the README's verification section.
